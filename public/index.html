The problem

About 2 million college-educated immigrants in the US work in jobs below their skills, or can't find work at all. Migration Policy Institute, “Untapped Talent” report

Most of them don't lack skills. They lack a map: which US job matches their experience, whether that job needs a license, how to describe their work the way US employers read it, and how to answer interview questions in a second language.

Career centers are free but booked for weeks, and they can't sit with you at 11 pm after a shift. CareerPort can.

How it works

  1. Tell us what you did back home

    A short form about your profession, education and English. Write in your own language if that's easier. It takes about three minutes.

  2. See three US paths

    A fast start, your real profession and a higher ceiling, each with pay, timing, cost and license rules. Plus a plan for this week that fits your free hours.

  3. Get ready to be hired

    A US resume for the role you pick, a fit check with a tailored resume and cover note for any job post you paste, and a spoken mock interview with corrected English.
